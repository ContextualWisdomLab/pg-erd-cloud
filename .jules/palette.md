## 2025-03-09 - Accessible Hints on Disabled Buttons
**Learning:** Replaced the native `disabled` attribute with `aria-disabled="true"` on a disabled button that uses `aria-describedby` to provide helpful context (like a tooltip or access management instructions). The native `disabled` attribute removes the element from the document's tab order, making it impossible for keyboard-only and screen reader users to focus it and discover why it is disabled.
**Action:** Always prefer `aria-disabled="true"` when a disabled button needs to convey a descriptive message or state reason to assistive technologies, and ensure CSS accommodates the `[aria-disabled="true"]` selector alongside `:disabled`.
